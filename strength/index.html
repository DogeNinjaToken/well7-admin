Operating notes

  • Before listing, review token transfer taxes, rebasing, pause/blacklist controls and administrative privileges.
  • Approve route assets and pools in Manage → Route Configuration. The contract rejects unapproved route assets, venues, pools, recipients and expired or distant deadlines.
  • V2 LP tokens and V3 NFTs are liquidity custody positions only. Token staking pools accept ordinary ERC-20 assets.
  • V3 POL is full range for this release. Collected fees are held separately by the POL manager until an explicit reinvestment action.

Testnet contract behavior and mock-router tests do not establish production readiness. Review deployed addresses and live pool liquidity before signing.
